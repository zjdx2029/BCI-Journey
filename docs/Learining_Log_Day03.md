**Lessons learned from my first experience:**
- **Surface Tension**: Solder follows heat. I learned to heat **both the pin and the PCB pad first**, then touch the solder wire. Otherwise, the solder forms a ball and falls off.
- **Temperature Danger**: The soldering iron is extremely hot. Safety awareness is crucial.
- **Manual Skill**: Soldering is not mentally complex, but it demands **high manual dexterity and patience**.
- **Result**: The connection is strong even if it doesn't look perfectly smooth. It's functional.
