## 2. Hardware Issues & Hypothesis

**Observation**: The data I collected looked like rhythmic noise rather than a standard ECG signal.
![noise](/images/noise.png)
**Hardware Connection Problem**: During wiring, I found the Dupont wires easily come loose. The AD8232 board's LED is not even on.
**My Hypothesis**:
1.  **Most Likely**: The male pin headers on the AD8232 are too short, and the female Dupont connector sockets are too deep. They are not making physical contact. (Explains why the board has no power).
2.  **Less Likely**: The AD8232 board itself is defective.

## 3. Action Plan (Solutions)

**Solution for Connection** (Emergency orders placed today):
1.  **Bridge with Male-to-Male wires**: Use male-to-male Dupont wires as a bridge, and secure the connection with hot melt glue.
2.  **Use a Breadboard**: Mount the AD8232 on a 400-hole breadboard and use jumper wires to connect.
**Next Steps**: Once the parts arrive, I will start troubleshooting. If it still doesn't work, I will replace the AD8232 board. I will record this as Issue #4 in my [Pitfalls.md](./Pitfalls.md).
